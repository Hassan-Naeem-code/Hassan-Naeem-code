Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

hassan-naeem.com is the personal portfolio of Muhammad Hassan Naeem. This policy covers two parts of the domain separately: the portfolio pages themselves, and the two live demos hosted under /vitalens and /emotion-modeling.

The portfolio pages

The portfolio (/, /privacy, and the files under /assets) is a static website. It has no backend, database, accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, or tracking.

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics or tracking pixels are loaded.
  • There are no forms, so nothing you type is collected.
  • All fonts, icons, stylesheets, and scripts are self-hosted. Your browser does not contact any third-party server while loading these pages.

The live demos

The two demos run on this domain but are separate applications. Because they use your camera and some third-party services, they work differently from the portfolio pages.

VitaLens (/vitalens)

  • Camera video and all body, face, and hand tracking are processed on your device. Video is never uploaded.
  • If you choose to send a photo for a health check, that photo is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate an answer. VitaLens does not store it. Anthropic processes it under its own privacy policy.
  • Your past health-check results are kept only in your browser's local storage, and you can clear them at any time.
  • To prevent abuse, your IP address (or a value derived from it) is used as a short-lived rate-limit key in Upstash Redis.
  • Anonymous performance measurements are collected with Vercel Speed Insights and a first-party performance endpoint. They contain no camera data or photos.
  • Model and anatomy files may be downloaded from Google Cloud Storage.

Emotion Modeling (/emotion-modeling)

  • Camera frames are analysed entirely in your browser by an ONNX model. No frames leave your device.
  • The ONNX Runtime and MediaPipe libraries are served from this domain. The face-detection model is downloaded from Google Cloud Storage, which receives standard request data such as your IP address and user-agent.

Hosting

The site and the demos are hosted on Vercel. As the hosting provider, Vercel processes standard server access logs (IP address, user-agent, timestamp, requested path) to deliver the site and prevent abuse. These logs are governed by Vercel's own privacy policy.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

I don't keep any personal data from the portfolio pages, and the demos don't store your photos or camera data. Anything a demo keeps in your browser can be removed by clearing this site's data. If you have a question or a request, contact me through the links on the portfolio's contact section.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS with HSTS preload. The portfolio pages send a strict Content Security Policy, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy, and anti-framing headers. The demos send their own security headers, tailored to their camera and WebAssembly needs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
